# War on the Rocks Article

Software development is a critical capability for the modern military. Despite the ease of access to technology and the ubiquity of software in the commercial sector, the military has struggled to allow for the same pace of rapid development for software applications. Collin, Drew, and Will, all members of Marine Coders, discuss how the Marine Corps Reserve is uniquely positioned to help close this gap in a recent article published in [War on the Rocks](https://warontherocks.com/2025/08/how-reservists-can-jumpstart-military-software-development/). They discuss real world projects which have been successfully delivered by reservists, the unique benefits of leveraging reservists for software development, and recommendations for how the Marine Corps can better utilize this untapped resource.

Read the full article [here](https://warontherocks.com/2025/08/how-reservists-can-jumpstart-military-software-development/).
